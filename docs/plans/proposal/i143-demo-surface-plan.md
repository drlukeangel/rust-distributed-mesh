# The demo as the proof of RDM's surface: what rafka-v2's node-admin and mesh layer do today, the RDM surface each part moves onto, what the demo verifies, and what to add

Luke, 2026-10-20: node-admin does a lot of work for Rafka today and most of it moves onto RDM in i141/i142. Verify at the
POC level, in the demo and before any app code is attached, that RDM's APIs and mechanisms are there for it, then run
chaos over them, so that a defect found later with the app attached is the app's and not RDM's (the way it is never iroh's).
The demo does not become Rafka's node-admin; that is i141/i142. It verifies the surface.

## 3. The chaos the suite does not run yet

The suite today: the A/B UDP cut with heal, SIGKILL, SIGSTOP, container pause and kill, on roles and non-primary admins;
restart, replace, delete, hand-off; proxy cutback, carrier restart and edge loss, churn; control loss of one and two
admins; in both providers, deterministic (C1-C20) and randomised (S1-S5). The soak guards the fabric-primary from every
fault, refuses to silence its mesh, heals every cut on the next row and draws one action per round. The testkit's link
faults (subset partition, flapping link, inbound-only deafness), disk pressure, gossip chunk loss, connection faults and
node-admin stalls at a named cut reach RDM's own gate and never the consumer executables. Excluded by Luke: the
fabric-primary killed during the first Build (Q74).

- **X1 authority loss.** The fabric-primary killed once the first Build is complete, and mid-Build on a later one; wedged, released inside the floor and held past the hung-hold floors; its mesh cut from the rest, and it alone cut from everyone, with a Build submitted on each side. Both admins of one mesh killed together; every admin of the fabric killed together (fabric-recovery.md). The executing mesh-primary killed with a step in flight; a hand-off mid-Build; hand-offs back to back.
- **X2 duration past the floors.** A cut held through the staleness and hung-hold floors with traffic on both sides, then healed: same births. A wedge past the hung-hold floors (R-FM1 terminate and recover). A data dir removed while exited (R-FM2). Disk pressure on one node's data dir during traffic and during its restart.
- **X3 network shapes.** Subset partitions (a role from its own mesh, a gateway from its brokers, a three-way split); a flapping link on a schedule; inbound-only deafness; gossip chunk loss on app channels under traffic; latency, jitter and loss percentage by netem on loopback (root, like the cut rules).
- **X4 storms.** Several faults per round across families; cuts that persist while kills and restarts land on both sides; a whole class killed at once; an admin and its mesh's gateway together; a restart of a node whose previous restart is still rejoining.
- **X5 lifecycle breadth.** Mesh create and delete under traffic, a mesh deleted while cut; node name, config and issuer updates with faults during them; the cert rows of C2; one node's clock skewed and drifted (D1); the last gateway of a mesh deleted while calls are carried through it.
- **X6 provider and host.** Container OOM kill and a daemon restart; an unwritable data dir; descriptor exhaustion on a gateway under churn; CPU starvation of an admin (slow, not wedged); the collector down for a soak window.
- **X7 scale.** A three-mesh estate and a larger count for one soak.

## 4. The plan, in order

Each item is one cell on the consumer executables first (proven, read from its evidence), then an action kind and a profile
in the soak model where it is a fault. The fabric-primary guard becomes a profile choice keyed on the first Build being
complete. Nothing here changes RDM product code unless a cell is red; a red is a product defect fixed RED-first, or a
page gap asked as a ruling.

1. **B1 + C1** (one landing; D1 is Q87, landed first): the consumer executables carry the chain; every existing soak then proves the pull, the cert and the time under chaos through readiness, the wedge and cut stories now through the heard-again re-entry. `demo/tests/projections.rs` gains the rafka-shaped cell (an admin declaring nothing, certs on).
2. **A1 + E1 + A2**: a role binds one app listener slot, is ready only once it answers, and reads its `config`.
3. **G1**: app work at state-commit and lifecycle hooks at mesh-create and node-delete, with chaos on them.
4. **X1** authority loss as C stories, then the S6 profile; **X2** long holds as C stories, then in S6.
5. **C2 + X5**: cert lifecycle and lifecycle breadth, the S7 profile with short TTLs.
6. **X3 + X4**: link faults and storms, the S8 profile; netem is new tooling and lands after X3's existing families.
7. **I1 + I2 + K1**: the UI's mesh and fabric flows, a Rafka-sized reply, the storage-fault family.
8. **X6 + X7**: provider and host faults, scale.
9. Record: the readiness matrix rows ([i143-ingestion-readiness.md](i143-ingestion-readiness.md) §2) updated per landing; rafka-v2's i141 decision 4, gap G22 and the i142 entry-pull row point at this page and §11 of the migration notes.

## 5. Decisions asked of Luke (one at a time, recommendation first, when each comes up)

- **D1** resolved (Luke, 2026-10-20, Q87): the invariant is stronger than a time reading: every entry to ready-for-traffic from any other state has that birth's hook attempt (`rdm.mesh.node.update.via-before-ready-attempt`) before it, where the node takes its cert, its time and its cache from the admin that accepted it; read from RDM's own evidence, no new op. A birth heard again after a hold or a cut is commanded back through node.mesh.join and runs the sequence again (`node/node_heard_again.rs`).
- **D2** one endpoint and one pooled connection per peer birth in RDM, against Rafka's striped endpoints and slots: accept, or rafka-v2 e9's load proof first. Recommended: accept; e9 reads the pool.
- **D3** a member's span level at runtime: gone with its surface. Recommended.
- **D4** an executable update: a page line, or Rafka's rolling restart over node.restart. Recommended: Rafka's own, over node.restart, no page.
- **D5** node-admin stalls at a named cut in the consumer: the demo's node-admin takes the testkit decorators under a test-only feature, or the stalls stay RDM's gate. Recommended: stay RDM's; the consumer composition is the product's shape.
