# Span name census (rafka-v2 #3206, report)

Every RDM span name checked against `docs/eng/telemetry-patterns.md` §8, and every finding fixed
on branch `i143-3206-span-renames`. Standard: §8, from `docs/architecture/telemetry.md:20`,
`node-rpc-envelope.md` §Runtime spans, `node-identity-and-state.md` §9, rafka-v2
`docs/eng/observability-conventions.md`, and the vocabulary bans in `CLAUDE.md` with
`scripts/name-check.py`'s set. Checked per name: owner `rdm`; five segments; component one of
`node_admin`, `node_rpc`, `mesh`, `node`, `testkit`; one-word snake_case entity; verb on the
twelve-word whitelist; reason kebab-case with `via-` or `from-`; no banned word in any segment; a
literal at its own span macro.

## Method

- Generated list: `docs/reference/rdm/spans.md`, regenerated by `rdm-docgen --root <lane> --out
  <dir>` (scanner `tools/mesh-audit/src/telemetry.rs::emitters`: the non-test source of `crates/`,
  `tools/`, `demo/`, read with `syn`).
- Independent scan: a regex pass over the same non-test source for every `*_span!` name,
  `#[instrument(name = ..)]`, `concat!` name and every `"rdm.<component>.<entity>.<verb>.<reason>"`
  string literal, compared both ways with the generated list.
- Test-only spans (inside `#[cfg(test)]` or `tests/`) are out of the census, as in the scanner.

## How each family was renamed

- **Node RPC op and step-event spans** (`crates/rdm-core/src/names.rs` and their server sides). The
  entity is what the op acts on, the sub-word of a six-segment name: `node.connections.*` is
  `connection`, `node.config.*` `config`, `node.tags.*` `tag`, `node.name.update` `name`,
  `node.topology.get` `topology`, `node.primary.update` and `node.fabric-primary.update` `primary`;
  `node.mesh.leave` removes a node from its mesh (`node.remove`). Every `get` is `resolve` (a lookup
  that produces an answer; the call span keeps its `outcome`). The stop and start workflows change a
  node's status: `node.update.via-stop-workflow`, `…via-start-workflow`. A step event's frame span is
  `<entity>.<verb>.via-<event>-frame`. Op and event names on the wire are unchanged.
- **Banned words.** `seat` is the primary (`primary.update`, `via-primary-changed`,
  `via-fabric-primary-changed`); the successor's status fold is `via-successor-fold`
  (node-cert-renew.md:65, "A successor primary folds"); `departed` is the wire's `deleted`
  (`MeshTopology.deleted`) or build.md §5.2's "departure"; `fenced` is the code's
  `incarnation_deleted`; `fence` is `investigate_path`'s path investigation; `retired` is `removed`;
  `authority` is `fabric-primary` (the topology door's refusal is `via-fabric-primary-required`,
  distinct from the build door's existing `via-not-fabric-primary`) or `not-ready` (an admin held
  Pending by a runtime it cannot manage); `partition` is the fault's cut.
- **Components.** `fabric.issuer` is node-admin's mint of a mesh's next issuing key:
  `node_admin.issuer.create.via-renewal`, distinct from the per-admin
  `node_admin.issuer.update.via-renewal`. `scenario.wedge` is the test kit's (`testkit`).
  `store.open` (four segments) is the node's store open by folding its file: `node.store.resolve`.
- **Literals.** `reject_span` (`crates/rdm-admin/src/control_plane.rs`) names each of its 22 reasons
  and its `via-invalid-intent` fallback as its own literal arm.
